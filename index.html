05 — Troubleshooting

Quick Reference

  • Small breakouts in the first 4–6 weeks: normal, continue the ramp
  • New breakouts in new spots, or worse after week 2: drop back a step on the ramp
  • Stinging past 60 seconds: skin wasn't fully dry — wait longer after cleansing
  • Dryness or flaking: more Aestura, or every-other-night until it settles
  • A bump or pit you can feel, not just see: stop, see a dermatologist

06 — Tracking

Photos, Every Two Weeks

  • Day 0, then every 2 weeks through week 12
  • Straight on plus both three-quarter angles, same light, same distance
  • Week 4: fewer new breakouts, marks about the same — normal
  • Week 8: oldest marks visibly lighter compared to day 0
  • Week 12: decision point — continue, or talk to a dermatologist about a prescription option
